Gramly Privacy Policy

Effective date: 31 October 2026

Gramly is a calculator app published by Kirtidhwaj Patra. Calculations, saved items, and calculation history are stored on your device. We do not receive those entries through the purchase system. You can use the calculator without creating an account, and calculations work offline.

If you open the paywall, buy Gramly Pro, or restore a purchase, the app uses Apple's App Store and RevenueCat to show available products, process purchases, and determine whether Pro is active. RevenueCat creates an anonymous app user ID and processes App Store purchase history and technical information needed to provide the purchase service. Apple processes payment information; Gramly does not receive your payment card details. RevenueCat may use purchase information for its service analytics. See RevenueCat's privacy policy and Apple's privacy policy.

We do not use purchase information for third-party advertising or track you across apps for advertising. We do not sell your calculation entries. Local notification permission is requested only if you choose a trial reminder; you can change it in iOS Settings.

You can manage or cancel an auto-renewing subscription through your Apple Account's subscription settings. Use Restore purchases in Gramly to recover access tied to your Apple Account. To ask about this policy or purchase data, contact mrswiftui@gmail.com.

This policy may change if the app's data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
